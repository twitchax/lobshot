# Summary
Two people play Coilback against each other in two browsers, by link, on Tidewall, with the full
HUD, weather, chat, spectators, reconnect and kicks; a bot can take a seat.

# Problem
M1 makes a game that only runs headless. M2 makes it playable online, and it is the first point
where real feel (movement prediction, hold-space power, the HUD) can be judged.

# Goals
1. A full 1v1 game over localhost in two tabs, and against SearchBot.
2. Reconnecting mid-game puts a player back in their seat.
3. The art pipeline is Rust (`cargo xtask art`), and the first mobile, rider and map are finished.

# Technical Approach
See `DESIGN.md`: "Protocol", "Rooms", "Bots and the lab", "Client" and "Art". The server broadcasts
accepted intents and its rolls; every client replays them with the shared `sim`, and per-turn
state hashes catch desync (a full snapshot repairs it).

# Assumptions
- `ewebsock` works with Bevy 0.20 on wasm32.

# Constraints
- Bevy 0.20, pinned; WebGL2.
- Performance targets are measured and reported here, not gated.

# References to Code
- `crates/server/src/server.rs` (router), `crates/client/`, `assets/`, `docs/research/art-spike/scripts/`.

# Non-Goals (MVP)
- Teams, items, other mobiles, sudden death, deployment: M3.
