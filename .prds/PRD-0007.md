# Summary
GunBound's other three modes.

# Problem
Solo is one of four GunBound modes; "all of the GunBound modes" was the call.

# Goals
1. Score, Tag and Jewel are room options, each playable with humans and bots.

# Technical Approach
Modes are rules in `sim` over the same turn order, so replays, bots and the lab work unchanged.

# Assumptions
- The research's GunBound mode rules (mechanics.md section 7) are accurate enough; gaps get a
  recorded pick.

# Constraints
- None beyond M5's.

# References to Code
- `crates/sim/`.

# Non-Goals (MVP)
- DragonBound-only modes (Battle Royale, Boss).

# History
- 2026-10-19: Created from DESIGN.md's milestones.
- 2026-10-24: Active. T-001 (Score) goes first and lays what every mode shares: the mode as a room
  option, the game's end decided per mode, and respawn at a picked drop point. Tag and Jewel follow
  on it once M5's roster has merged, so Jewel's no-damage rule covers every new shot. Each mode
  brings its own share of T-004 (bots playing the objective).
- 2026-10-24: T-001 done: Score. Lives = players + 1 (SW-modes, GF-tiro, GF-vgg, GF-kurai); the
  drop lands at the end of the 4th turn after the pick's (SW-modes over GF-vgg's count from the
  pick); the returning mobile's turn is the second after it lands (GF-super). Picks: a forfeit
  costs a life, the drop's wind is a quarter of a shot's, full HP on return. Wire and protocol
  versions went to 4. A browser test drops a dead player back in by clicking the map, and found
  a double-started clock after walking off the map, fixed for Solo too.
- 2026-10-25: T-002 done: Tag. Classic's rule (out when the mobile in play dies) over DragonBound's
  (the backup comes in), which no archived DB guide confirms. Picks: a switch costs nothing and
  can't buy a second walk; nothing changes on the bench; one loadout per player; Random rolls
  each pick. SearchBot switches when its mobile is low or the other has a much better shot.
- 2026-10-25: T-003 and T-004 done: Jewel, and with it the bots of every mode. Picks where the
  sources are silent: 4 jewels at the start and one a turn up to 8, a 6:2:1:1 mix, straight down a
  rolled column with no wind, the Onyx turning after 2 rounds, and the team whose turn it is
  scoring. A Normal bot takes a jewel about one turn in eight, so bot games to 100 run long:
  for Aaron.
